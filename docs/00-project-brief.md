# Project Brief

## One-Line Description

Growth Lab is a small-business online growth command center for an arts retail brand with strong organic reach, weak non-seasonal online sales, and limited operator capacity.

## User / Customer

Primary user: a solo or part-time online marketer working with a stretched local retail, arts, or community brand.

Secondary users: founders, shop managers, or community organisation leads who need to see whether online marketing is working without managing the details themselves.

## Primary Outcome

The user can turn a vague growth mandate into a concrete 6-month plan, weekly actions, tracked experiments, and clear evidence about what should be stopped, continued, or scaled.

## Non-Goals

- Do not build a job application, CV generator, or hiring workflow.
- Do not spend real ad budget or connect to live ad accounts in the first version.
- Do not require Shopify, GA4, Meta, Instagram, or email-provider credentials for the demo.
- Do not optimize for generic enterprise marketing operations.
- Do not make a landing page as the main experience.

## Constraints

- Technical: start with a local demo app and realistic seed data; keep integration boundaries clear for later.
- Product: focus on practical workflows a one-day-per-week operator could actually use.
- Time: build in stages, with a usable first version before adding automation depth.
- Budget: reflect a tiny marketing budget, specifically a staged organic-first month and a small paid budget from month 2 onward.
- Legal/security/privacy: avoid real customer data, account credentials, or live tracking in the first version.

## Success Criteria

- A user can understand the brand's current growth position from the dashboard within 60 seconds.
- A user can create, review, and update marketing experiments with hypothesis, channel, audience, creative, spend, result, and next action.
- The app can compare a current month against the same month last year and calculate the 25 percent growth threshold plus commission above that threshold.
- The app can show a 6-month plan with month 1 audit/organic work and months 2-6 paid/organic campaign testing.
- The app can generate or mock useful campaign copy, captions, bundle ideas, and weekly recommendations from the seeded brand context.
- The final UI feels like a real operating tool for a small arts retail business, not a generic SaaS template.
