### 2026-06-04 - Build Growth Lab As A Demo-First Operating Tool

Status: Accepted

Context: The source research describes a small arts retail brand with organic following, low non-seasonal online sales, limited staff capacity, a 6-month test horizon, and a tiny paid budget.

Decision: Build a working web app that models the marketing operating system: dashboard, campaign planner, experiment tracker, audience strategy, creative library, growth math, and weekly recommendations. Start with realistic mocked data instead of live integrations.

Options considered: a job-application response, a static strategy document, a generic analytics dashboard, or a full integration-heavy marketing platform.

Consequences: The first version can be built, tested, and shown quickly while still being technically challenging. Commerce, analytics, ads, social, and email integrations remain future work.
